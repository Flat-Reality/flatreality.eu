3. DATA WE PROCESS

3.1. Core game and essential diagnostics

We may process IP address, pseudonymous technical identifiers, platform, device model or characteristics, operating system, language, Game version, timestamps, security logs, error information and minimum diagnostics. We do not intend to use this layer to build advertising profiles or analyse individual narrative choices.

3.2. FR Connect, XBOX® and cloud services

When you activate FR Connect we may receive or generate XBOX® user ID/XUID, gamertag, avatar or avatar URL, PlayFab ID where PlayFab is enabled in that product build, linked platform identifiers, sign-in state, achievements, progress, save files, synchronisation metadata, quota, last-modified time and logs needed to provide and protect the service.

We do not receive your Microsoft/XBOX® password. Authentication uses Microsoft interfaces. A product build that does not include PlayFab will not send data to PlayFab merely because that provider is listed in this Policy.

3.3. Voluntary game analytics

If you consent, Unity Analytics or another tool identified in the consent notice may receive a pseudonymous identifier, device and session data, Game version, approximate country derived from IP, launch/close events, performance, errors and interaction or progress events designed by Flat Reality. We will not use these events to collect free text, private communications or special-category data.

3.4. Website and cookies

When you visit our sites we process HTTP requests, IP address, user-agent, timestamps and security logs needed to deliver and protect the site. Your browser stores a local preference to remember cookie choices.

With consent, Google Analytics may process identifiers and browsing data to measure use and performance, and Microsoft Clarity may process page interactions, events, device data and session recordings using available masking controls. Google Tag Manager manages tags but does not itself authorise an optional category. FR Channel articles and assets are delivered through Supabase; access to public content may produce technical delivery logs.

3.5. Communications

If you contact Flat Reality, we process your email address, name, message, attachments, message metadata and information needed to reply, verify identity or resolve a request. Do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary.

If you separately opt in while submitting a Partners inquiry, we may use your name and email address to send marketing news about opportunities for Flat Reality Partners. This is optional, does not affect our response to your inquiry, and may be withdrawn at any time through the unsubscribe method in a message or by contacting legal@flatreality.eu.

3.6. Purchases and platforms

Stores process payments and normally do not disclose full payment-card details to us. We may receive transaction identifiers, country or region, product, licence, entitlement status, refunds and fraud-prevention information needed to deliver purchases, DLC or support.

4. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES

- Run the game and provide requested features. Data: licence, device and configuration. Legal basis: performance of contract.
- FR Connect, cloud/cross-save and achievements. Data: platform identity, progress, files and logs. Legal basis: performance of the service requested on connection.
- Security, fraud prevention and minimum diagnostics. Data: IP, identifiers, errors and logs. Legal basis: legitimate interests in protecting and maintaining the service.
- Expanded game analytics. Data: events, device and session. Legal basis: consent.
- Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. Data: cookies/identifiers, browsing and interaction. Legal basis: consent.
- Respond to requests and exercise rights. Data: contact, message and proof of identity. Legal basis: contract, legal obligation or legitimate interests, depending on the request.
- Accounting, claims and compliance. Data: relevant transactions and communications. Legal basis: legal obligation and establishment or defence of legal claims.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we assess necessity, proportionality and impact and provide a right to object. We do not make a contract conditional on consent to processing that is not necessary for it.

5. CONSENT AND CONTROLS

Accepting an EULA is not consent to analytics. Optional controls are separate, off by default and explain the purpose before collection begins. You may withdraw consent through game settings or the website’s “Cookie settings”. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Website categories are:

- Essential: security, delivery, load balancing and storage of your choice; always active.
- Preferences: remember non-essential site choices; require consent where the law requires it.
- Analytics: Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity; off until consent.
- Marketing: campaign or advertising tags if added in future; off until consent and currently not used to sell data.

6. CHILDREN

Our games are offered according to their age ratings. In Spain, FR Connect and voluntary consent-based processing are not available to children under 14 without verifiable authorisation from a parent or guardian. If we cannot offer a suitable authorisation mechanism, those features remain disabled. Parents or guardians may contact legal@flatreality.eu to request review or deletion.

In other countries, we apply the local age of digital consent and contract rules. We do not knowingly collect a child’s data in breach of those rules.

9. RETENTION

- Voluntary game analytics using Unity Analytics: raw event data is retained for 13 months and then automatically deleted. Dashboard metrics and player profiles are retained until you request deletion or Flat Reality initiates their deletion. You can disable analytics and request deletion through the Game's Settings; disabling analytics alone does not delete previously collected data. A shorter retention period applies where specifically configured and stated at collection.
- Voluntary web analytics: up to 12 months, unless a shorter setting is stated at collection.
- FR Connect, progress and cloud saves: while the account remains connected or until a valid deletion/disconnection request, subject to legal obligations and platform limitations.
- Essential security and diagnostic logs: for the minimum documented period needed to investigate incidents and maintain the service; reviewed periodically and not repurposed for voluntary analytics.
- Enquiries: while needed to reply and afterwards for applicable claim periods.
- Legal, tax and transaction records: for the periods required by applicable law.
- Backups: overwritten or isolated during a limited technical period; deleted data is not restored to ordinary use.

We may retain anonymised information that can no longer reasonably be linked to a person.

10. SECURITY

We use organisational and technical measures proportionate to risk, including minimisation, access controls, encryption in transit, environment separation, updates and provider review. No system is completely secure. If a breach creates a legally reportable risk, we will notify the authority and affected people as required.
